Who we are

A finance operations company, built for the long term.

TrueNorth Financial Services — carried on by 17325053 Canada Inc. — was established to provide accurate, dependable and technology-enabled bookkeeping and finance operations services that let clients make informed business decisions, built on long-term relationships of integrity, accountability and professional excellence.

Why we exist

Growing businesses are often too large for spreadsheets and too early for a full internal finance department. TrueNorth exists to close that gap with structured, recurring finance support rather than one-off transactional help.

Our service philosophy

Every service delivered is guided by accuracy, timeliness, professionalism, integrity, confidentiality, continuous improvement and client success — the standard set out in our internal charter.

Our governance

TrueNorth operates under a documented governance charter covering ethical leadership, accountability, risk management and separation of corporate and personal interests — reviewed by the Board at least once every financial year.

Our long-term vision

To become one of the most trusted independent finance operations and bookkeeping organizations — empowering businesses through dependable financial stewardship, responsible technology and sustainable practices.

Technology & automation

AI-powered finance operations.

TrueNorth combines experienced finance professionals with intelligent automation. Our charter treats artificial intelligence as a business enablement tool — not a substitute for professional judgment.

Where AI helps

Workflow automation, data analysis, financial reporting assistance, bookkeeping support and productivity enhancement.

Where humans decide

Material decisions affecting clients — financial reporting, client advice, tax calculations and financial statements — always receive appropriate human review before reliance.
