About this summary

TrueNorth Financial Services operates under a formal Corporate Governance Charter adopted by its Board. This page is a plain-language public summary of that charter, published so clients, partners and stakeholders can see the standards we hold ourselves to and check our work against them.

Where this summary quotes the charter directly, the article number is shown. If anything here differs from the charter itself, the charter governs. Clients and prospective clients can request the relevant sections by emailing jewelnme.001@gmail.com.

Who we are

TrueNorth Financial Services is the operating name of 17325053 Canada Inc., a Canadian corporation. We provide technology-enabled bookkeeping and finance operations support to businesses in Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom and Australia.

The charter applies to every Founder, Director, Officer, employee and contractor who acts for the Corporation. It is not optional guidance: the core values and conduct standards it sets out are adopted as mandatory.

Board oversight and annual review

The Board is responsible for the charter and for overseeing how it is applied. At minimum, the Board:

  • Reviews the charter at least once every financial year, and more often when laws, technology or the business materially change.
  • Oversees the information security, privacy and AI governance framework and reviews it periodically.
  • Receives reports on significant risks, incidents and any matters raised under the concerns process.
  • Approves material changes to policies that affect how client work is delivered.

Ethical leadership and conduct

Everyone acting for TrueNorth is expected to conduct business honestly, ethically and professionally in every engagement. In practice that means representing our services accurately, never overstating qualifications or capabilities, dealing fairly with clients and suppliers, and refusing any instruction that would require acting unlawfully or dishonestly.

Accountability

Each client engagement has a named finance operations specialist who is accountable for the work, rather than an anonymous queue. Responsibility can be delegated, but accountability cannot: the person who delegates a task remains responsible for its outcome, and decisions and actions are documented so they can be traced and reviewed.

Separation of corporate and personal interests

The charter requires a clear separation between the Corporation's interests and the personal interests of its people. Corporate funds, assets and information are used only for corporate purposes. Anyone with a personal interest that could conflict with a client's or the Corporation's interests must disclose it and step back from the related decision.

Risk management

We identify, assess and manage the risks that could affect clients or the continuity of our service, including data security, key-person dependency, errors in financial records, supplier and platform failure, and regulatory change. Controls are defined before client transactions start flowing, and are revisited as each engagement and the business grow.

Service philosophy

“Every service delivered shall be guided by accuracy, timeliness and professional excellence.”

Service Philosophy, Article 6.3

Alongside accuracy and timeliness, the charter names professionalism, integrity, confidentiality, continuous improvement and client success as the standards every piece of work is measured against. We prefer long-term, recurring relationships over one-off transactional work, because that is how finance operations become genuinely dependable.

Confidentiality and information security

“Confidential information shall be protected using commercially reasonable safeguards.”

Confidentiality, Article 7.4

Client financial information is among the most sensitive data a business holds. Our safeguards include:

  • Access management: access to client systems and records is limited to the people who need it for the engagement, and removed when it is no longer needed.
  • Incident reporting and response: suspected security or privacy incidents must be reported immediately and are handled under a documented response procedure, including notifying affected clients where required.
  • Awareness training: everyone associated with the Corporation receives periodic information-security awareness training.
  • Approved platforms only: client data is handled in the accounting, payroll and storage platforms approved for the engagement.

See our Privacy Policy for how personal information is collected, used and protected.

Responsible use of AI

“Artificial Intelligence [is] a business enablement tool rather than a substitute for professional judgment.”

AI Governance Principles, Article 27.12

The charter permits artificial intelligence to be used responsibly, transparently and lawfully to make our work faster and more consistent. It does not permit AI to replace the judgment of a qualified professional.

Any AI-assisted output that could materially affect financial reporting, tax calculations, financial statements or client advice receives appropriate human review before it is relied on.

Professional boundaries

We are clear about what we do not do. Nothing in our services authorizes regulated public accounting, audit, assurance, legal, investment or tax representation services unless appropriately licensed. Where work such as GST/HST or sales tax filing support requires it, we prepare the information for review by the client's accountant or tax advisor.

Sustainability

We operate digital-first: paperless records, cloud platforms and remote delivery. This reduces travel, printing and physical storage, and is part of how we minimize our environmental impact while serving clients across four countries.

Raising a concern

Clients, suppliers, employees and contractors can raise a concern about conduct, confidentiality or any apparent breach of this charter by emailing jewelnme.001@gmail.com with the subject line “Governance concern”. Concerns are acknowledged, reviewed without retaliation against anyone raising them in good faith, and reported to the Board where they are significant.

Changes to this summary

We update this summary when the charter changes. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last reviewed.

Ten values, held as standards.

These are not aspirations. Our governance charter adopts them as mandatory standards of conduct for every Founder, Director, Officer, employee and contractor. Here is what each one looks like in your engagement.

Adopted in Governance charterApplies to Everyone at TrueNorthValues 10

Integrity

We conduct business honestly, ethically and professionally in every engagement.

  • We describe our services and their limits accurately.
  • We tell you about errors we find, including our own.
  • We decline work we cannot do properly.

Accountability

We accept responsibility for our decisions, our actions and the work we delegate.

  • A named specialist owns your engagement.
  • Work is documented so it can be traced and reviewed.
  • Delegating a task never means handing off responsibility.

Reliability

Commitments made to clients, suppliers and stakeholders are fulfilled to the greatest extent reasonably possible.

  • Month-end close is delivered on the day we commit to.
  • We respond within one business day.
  • If a date is at risk, you hear it from us early.

Confidentiality

Confidential information is protected using commercially reasonable safeguards.

  • Access limited to the people on your engagement.
  • Client data stays on approved platforms.
  • Incidents are reported and handled under a documented procedure.

Professional excellence

We pursue continuous learning, process improvement and high-quality service delivery.

  • Every statement is reviewed before it reaches you.
  • Our team keeps current on the platforms we support.
  • We measure quality, not just output.

Innovation

We adopt automation and artificial intelligence responsibly, with appropriate human oversight.

  • Automation removes repetitive work from your month.
  • AI output is never relied on without review.
  • New tools are evaluated before they touch client data.

Sustainability

We minimize our environmental impact through digital-first operations and efficient use of resources.

  • Paperless records and cloud platforms.
  • Remote delivery across four countries.
  • Efficient processes that waste less time and energy.

Respect

We foster a workplace that values professionalism, inclusion and mutual respect.

  • Clear, courteous communication with every client.
  • Fair treatment of our people and suppliers.
  • Room for questions, at any level of financial knowledge.

Client-centric

We work to understand client objectives and deliver practical, commercially valuable solutions.

  • We start by learning how your business makes money.
  • Reports are built around the decisions you need to make.
  • Scope grows with you, not with our sales targets.

Continuous improvement

Our policies, technologies and governance practices are reviewed periodically for long-term resilience.

  • Annual Board review of our governance charter.
  • Periodic process reviews within every engagement.
  • Client feedback turned into concrete changes.

Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how 17325053 Canada Inc., operating as TrueNorth Financial Services (“TrueNorth”, “we”, “us”), collects, uses, discloses and protects personal information. It covers this website and the bookkeeping and finance operations services we provide to businesses in Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom and Australia.

For questions about this policy or your personal information, contact our Privacy Officer at jewelnme.001@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Enquiry form: your name, work email, company, market and the service you are interested in.
  • Correspondence: anything you include when you email us or speak with us on a call.

Information we handle for clients

To deliver services, we process our clients’ financial records, which can include personal information about the client’s owners, employees, contractors, customers and suppliers. Examples include names, contact details, bank and payment transaction details, invoices, and, for payroll work, compensation, deductions and government identification numbers required for payroll reporting.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs: our hosting provider records standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and timestamps, used to keep the site secure and working.
  • Spam protection: our enquiry form includes a hidden field and records how long the form was open before it was sent. These are used only to filter automated spam.

How we use information

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a discovery call.
  • To prepare proposals and engagement letters.
  • To deliver the services set out in a client’s engagement letter.
  • To secure our systems, prevent fraud and filter spam.
  • To meet our legal, regulatory and record-keeping obligations.
  • To improve our services and processes.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Legal bases (UK and EU)

Where the UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies, we rely on: your consent when you submit an enquiry; performance of a contract when we deliver services; legal obligation for record-keeping and compliance; and our legitimate interests in running, securing and improving our business, balanced against your rights.

Client data we process on your behalf

When we handle personal information inside a client’s financial records, the client decides why that information is processed, and we act as their service provider (a “processor” under UK and EU law). We process it only to deliver the agreed services, under the client’s instructions and our engagement terms, and we help clients respond to requests from the individuals concerned.

Artificial intelligence and your data

Under our governance charter, client financial data is never placed on unauthorized platforms. AI tools are used only where approved for client work, and AI-assisted output that could affect financial reporting, tax calculations or advice is reviewed by a qualified person before it is relied on. We do not permit client data to be used to train public AI models.

Who we share information with

  • Service providers that help us operate, such as website hosting, email, secure file storage and communication tools, bound by confidentiality and data-protection obligations.
  • Platforms you choose, such as Xero, QuickBooks Online, Zoho Books, Wave, payroll systems and payment processors, when we work in them on your behalf.
  • Your advisors, such as your accountant or tax advisor, when you ask us to.
  • Authorities, where disclosure is required by law.
  • A successor business, if TrueNorth is reorganized, merged or sold, subject to this policy.

International transfers

We serve clients in several countries, and our service providers may store or process information in Canada, the United States or other countries. Personal information may therefore be subject to the laws of those countries. We use contractual and practical safeguards to keep it protected to the standard described in this policy.

How long we keep information

  • Enquiries that do not become engagements: up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Client records: for the length of the engagement, and afterwards for as long as the law requires. In Canada, business records must generally be kept for six years.
  • Server logs: for the period set by our hosting provider, typically short.

When information is no longer needed, we securely delete it or return it to the client.

How we protect information

We use commercially reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards, including role-based access, strong authentication on the platforms we use, encrypted connections, documented incident response and periodic security training. No system is perfectly secure, so if a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify affected clients and individuals and the relevant regulators as required by law.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. If we add analytics or other cookies in future, we will update this policy and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent first.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you, and receive a copy.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Withdraw consent, or object to certain processing.
  • Ask us to delete information, subject to our legal retention obligations.
  • Request that we restrict processing, or transfer your information (UK and EU).

These rights are provided in Canada under PIPEDA and applicable provincial laws, in the UK under the UK GDPR, in Australia under the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles, and in the US under applicable state privacy laws. To make a request, email us. We respond within the time the applicable law requires, usually 30 days, and may need to verify your identity first. If your information is held in a client’s records, we may refer your request to that client.

Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

Complaints

Please contact us first at jewelnme.001@gmail.com so we can try to resolve your concern. You also have the right to complain to your privacy regulator: the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, the UK Information Commissioner’s Office, or the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services, technology or the law change. The effective date at the top of the page shows the current version. For material changes, we will notify clients directly.
